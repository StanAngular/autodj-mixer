2026-11-21 Berlin Pulse (tech house), 126 BPM, A, 4:22, `workspace/music/berlin_pulse_2026_11_21/score.py`, sent with 8 layer clips; verdict pending, not heard by me. Style profile "house" (mood club), rolling offbeat bass (Plan.bass cell [2,6,10,14] between four-on-the-floor kicks), offbeat stabs (pianet) with a cell changing every 8 bars, sparse hook, arc + drum_plan_v2.
**Findings:**
- P147 can exhaust: with a 15-track window every consonant idiom (15) was taken -> HarmonyRepeat for all. The score now falls back to the jazz IDIOMS adapted to the scale, then colour +1/-1 (another quality = another progression). The engine should offer this itself (open).
- Cabasa/clave/etc. were judged as pitched sources by soundgate.report -> bad_sounds gate fired; fresh.CLASS perc now lists them.
- CLAP «snappy clap» returned a whip (Whiplash.wav) and a sharp electro clap; name veto + proposed-rule preference now steer away.
- Checks: gates passed, musicality PASS (key_fit 0.77, tonal clarity of the journeys 0.73-0.79), rhythm checklist 6/6 ok, kick_bass 0.12.

**Verdict 2026-11-21: total failure.** «Это не tech house и даже не близко. При чём тут conga, это лайт афрохаус? Ohat туда же (Cabasa). Keys хрень (pianet offbeat stabs). Полная херня, какой-то лайт афро lo-fi, отстой, а не тех хаус в архитектурном плане; всё подобрано хреново, сопливо, упрощённо.» He asked for a serious audit of what happened and to research what tech house actually is.
**Why (first read):** genre was only a rhythm profile ("house") + CLAP text queries; nothing defined the tech-house SOUND PALETTE or forbade afro/lofi families (congas, cabasa, clave, Rhodes-type stabs); freshness picked rare odd sounds; pianet stabs = lofi colour.
