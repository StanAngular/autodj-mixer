Verdict: old loop was better on the last two; new one "как будто хуже", maybe unfinished or style-specific. His diagnosis: the sound still smells of a synthesizer, and Shutter-style tracks lack samples/sounds. From then on he asks for tracks with "никакого синтезатора, только интересные свежие звуки, семплы" (Dream Organic House, deep garage breakbeat underground, progressive trance with industrial elements).

**How to apply:** default to recorded sources (VSCO harp/cello/viola/violin/Upright, acoustic kits, hand percussion, W.A. samples) and no synth voices unless he asks. Fresh harmony stays (chords.Plan). Do not claim "new engine is better": he compares by ear, old loop sometimes wins. Hypothesis test 2026-11-13: organic VSCO render vs old loop, his verdict pending.
