- **Text in tracks: Ukrainian or English only, never Russian** («ТЕКСТ НА УКР ИЛИ АНГЛИЙСКОМ ЯЗЫКЕ, НЕ НА РУССКОМ!!», 2026-10-24). TTS voices: uk-UA-OstapNeural / uk-UA-PolinaNeural or en-US/en-GB (check availability with `python3 -m edge_tts --list-voices`, PYTHONPATH=~/.local/lib/edgetts/pkgs).
- A constant background cymbal «ц-ц-ц» (offbeat shaker / hat / ride on all bars) annoys him: a cymbal layer should come and go (only some sections), rarely held all track.
- Syncopated short chord stabs repeating like a car horn («кто-то сигналит грубо, бибип бибип», Chord Shot next B) = bad; keep chord hits sparse/soft.
**Why:** direct feedback on Warm Acid act-2 A and Chord Shot next A/B.
**How to apply:** every voice insert: choose a uk/en voice; every arrangement: no always-on cymbal; no horn-like stab patterns.
- 2026-10-24 again: in Warm Acid (act 2 = second acid) he heard «тарелки опять» instead of the second acid: the RIDE was on in the peak sections (ride counts as a constant cymbal too) and the new acid line was under the hook. Rule: the element that defines a part must be the loudest new thing there; no ride/hat/shaker held through a whole part.

2026-11-18 Frequency: hats + open hats running all 128 bars by the tech house template = «фигня ненужная», removed in v2. Hats are not a default layer: add them only for a reason, and never for the whole track.
