2026-11-04, Scratch (ambient): Стас: bells came out muffled, less "волшебно звонко", and not stretched into a longer decay. Wanted: keep the shine of the original BellTree attack, only extend the tail (airier).

**Why:** I stretched the whole bell x2 and low-passed at 6.5 kHz, which smears the attack and kills the sparkle.
**How to apply:** keep the attack dry and bright (no LPF below ~12 kHz); lengthen the tail separately (reverb tail, frozen ring, layered stretched copy under the attack). Offer 3 bell-only versions to choose from. See [[feedback-no-recycled-gimmicks]].
