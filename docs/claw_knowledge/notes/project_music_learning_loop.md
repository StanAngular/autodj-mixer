2026-10-17 findings, all in /opt/autodj-mixer/autodj/compose:
- **Tuning bug:** Vital «Psychill Bass Clean» sounds an octave + ~a semitone low with a frequency shift; many Vital presets are off by whole tones/tritones. `tuning.py` measures each preset once (cache `~/samples/vital_presets/_tuning3.json`), transposes MIDI and flags `in_tune=False`. Стас heard it as layers that «не попадают гармонично»; Gemini did not catch it blind.
- **Clash gate:** `harmony.clashes` counts m2/m9 between layers (needs `trk.log_notes`); voicings now avoid internal m2/m9. «Тепло рук» v3: 34% -> 0%.
- **Gemini second ear** (`--review`): useful for palette/genre advice, but scores are noisy (same audio got groove 9 and 5) and it is suggestible: never put our own clash/defect numbers in its prompt, compare versions in one request rather than trusting absolute scores.
- **References:** Стас sends tracks via Dropbox -> `~/samples/references/<drop>/`, `python3 -m demucs -n htdemucs` (2.5 min per 7.5 min track on CPU), `python3 -m autodj.compose.learn STEMS NAME BPM` -> `~/samples/grooves/refs/NAME.json`, use with `groove.from_ref(NAME)`. First: Dorothy's Dream «The Blue Bus» (ro-minimal) -> «Нічний тролейбус».
- **Reference library (2026-10-17):** also learned Mike Morrisey & Ollie BC «Disfunktion», Bagheera «7 Heures du Matin», King Freak «Astrology», Sven Väth «Face It». - **Timbre matching:** the engine measures ~2800 drum hits from our packs and picks the closest to a reference's kick/hat/rim. Limit found on «Нічний тролейбус v2» (Gemini similarity 4/10): our packs are techno, 80s/90s machines and orchestra, so ro-minimal foley/micro-perc, dub chords and analog «rubber» bass can't be matched; asked Стас for minimal/microhouse one-shots, dub-chord presets, field recordings via Dropbox. 2026-10-17 Стас: the trolleybus beats are less dense and the other sounds worse than the reference, wants one track per reference with modern quality samples.

- **Reference tracks (2026-10-17):** `autodj/compose/reftrack.py` builds a track from a learned profile + config (5 tracks in `workspace/music/refs5/`). Measured with our own gate metrics, Стас's references are brighter (3-14 kHz -13..-20 dB vs our "шик" gate -19) and louder (RMS -9..-13, crest 8-13 dB) than our defaults, so for reference tracks set `META["gates"]["high_db"]` and `target` from the reference (a bit warmer/quieter), and `premaster_drive` 4-6 for club styles. Remixes (2026-10-17, Lasha Tumbai): Стас wants ONLY the original's sounds for vocals (no TTS), DJ Андрей: <=130 BPM, mostly the fun instrumental spots, «eins zwei» straight into the drop. Method that worked: demucs stems; Gemini on short labelled snippets (grounded) instead of whole-song timestamps (unreliable); beat grid by linear fit of beat_track on the drums stem; loops cut on original bars and `pedalboard.time_stretch`; use the ORIGINAL bass stem under its own loop and never layer two band loops (two harmonies rub); `timbre.tune_kick` puts the kick on the key root (W.A. kick was F# vs song in E).

Vocal chops from an original must be placed WORD BY WORD and verified (Стас 2026-10-17: «ты вслепую ложишь»). Method in workspace/music/lasha/score_v3.py: faster-whisper `small` with `language="de"` and an `initial_prompt` of the expected words gives usable word timestamps on the demucs vocal stem (plain auto-language ASR was useless); cut each word and trim it to its own attack (5 ms pre-roll), place on chosen 16ths, then re-detect onsets in the vocal bus and report offset per word (v3: 159 words, max 8 ms).

Lasha v7 lesson (2026-10-17): Стас heard the accordion as artefact-ridden, loud and dirty, and the extra layers (vocoder, gate, metal, sprinkles, swells) as junk, while Gemini called v7 "clean" — Gemini misses separation/stretch artefacts, trust Стас.
